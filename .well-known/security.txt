# KNOWN ISSUES
#
# These have been reported already, most of them by the world. Please do
# not report them again; it knows, and has decided to go on turning.
#
#  1. The world may be turned by anybody, in either direction, without
#     credentials. No fix is planned. It was turning before you came and
#     took your hand for weather.
#
#  2. Enter plants a flag in the middle of whatever is facing you. No
#     ownership of the land is checked, there being none to check. The
#     damage is limited to nine flags and to the one person who can see
#     them.
#
#  3. There is a hole at the middle of the picture. It was dug on purpose
#     and is open for inspection. A hole that is signposted is a feature,
#     and this is the signpost.
#
#  4. The At goes behind the world at regular intervals and cannot be
#     observed while it is there. What it does has not been audited. It
#     comes back looking the same, which auditors say is the worrying part.
#
#  5. An intruder who obtained everything the world holds on its visitors
#     would obtain nothing, that being what it holds. This has been
#     tested, by looking.
#
#  6. The speaking tube round the back answers whoever finds it. Privileges
#     cannot be escalated. There is one, and everybody has it.
#
#  7. The world is a little out of date at all times. This is the light's
#     fault and is being taken up with the light.
#
#  8. The Tilde Sea leaks, eastward, continuously. It has not got any
#     lower. Under investigation by the Commas, who are well placed.
#
