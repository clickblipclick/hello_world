A phrasebook

For travellers, of whom there are none. The local language is written with the same eight characters the place is made of, which saves on type but means that every sentence is also a small map.

The alphabet

#b, d, g, k. The hard sounds, made on high ground.
om, n, o, u, w. Anything that can be hummed.
:c, h, q, x. Said at dusk, with hesitation.
~a, e, l, r. The sounds that carry over water.
-f, s, t, v. The same, with less wind.
.i, j, y. Small, and said quietly.
*p, z. Reserved for the poles and for emphasis.
+Any digit. All numbers are written alike; see under arithmetic, in the gazetteer.
