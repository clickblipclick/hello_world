The desk

There is no border, the world being round and the rest being Elsewhere. But there is a desk at it, and the desk has a form. Nobody is behind the desk. The form is answered by the desk itself, which has had a long time to think about what it would say.

Name

Twenty-four characters. It will be converted into the local alphabet at the day’s rate, which is poor.

Purpose of visit
Length of stay

In turns of the world. Nine seconds each, near enough, unless somebody drags it.

Anything to declare 


    

    The permit prints, for those who like to carry one. It will not be asked for.
