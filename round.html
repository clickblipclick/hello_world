Once round

A game for two visitors, one die, and the middle of the world, which has been cut along the equator and laid out straight so that it can be raced on. It is thirty squares from the post to the post.




Two pieces have been set down at the post where longitude starts. They are
a lowercase i and a lowercase j, the j being an i with further to go down. The i throws first, having less to carry.





The rules


Throw, and go east by what the die says. East is the only direction; the world settled that by turning.
What happens next depends on what you are standing on, and happens once. First to come round to the post
again has been round the world, and wins. Nothing is won.

The board. The post is at both ends, being the same post.

  
#  the Hash

  
Dry land. You stay where you are put. It is the only square that can be relied on.

  
~  the Tilde Sea

  
Water goes east, and so do you, by one square. What you fetch up on is your affair and has no further effect.

  
.  the Deep

  
You go down, which is not east. The throw is struck from the record and you are back where you began it.

  
,  a Comma

  
Draw breath, which is what it is for, and throw again.

  
"  the Dittos

  
Cloud, meaning the same as the one above. Your throw is repeated, unseen, and you come out wherever that is.

  
^  the Caret

  
The ship moves one square east after every throw, whoever threw. End a throw on its square and you are
      taken aboard and carried until land is in sight, which is the square before the next Hash, where it
      puts you down and turns away. If land is already in sight you are put down at once, with an apology.




The die is marked in Deeps, there being no other way to write a small number here. A visitor alone may
play both hands. They will win, and also lose, and should decide beforehand which they came for.


No score is kept, and the pieces are put back at the post when you leave, by nobody.
