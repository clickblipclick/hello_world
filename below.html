Underneath

The world is drawn on the outside. This is the rest of it.

A hole was sunk at the middle of the picture, straight down, forty-four characters across, that being as wide as the world is and nobody having thought to make the hole narrower. What came up is laid out below in the order it was found. Depth is given in rows. The hole is not safe, but there is nothing in it that falls faster than you scroll.

That is the middle. It is not made of anything. It is the place where the next character would go, and the whole world has been set down around it, a row at a time, by something that has not yet decided what to say.

The hole stops here because the other half is the same thing the other way up. The way out is the way you came.
