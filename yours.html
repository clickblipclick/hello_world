Your own

The first one was made of two words. They were not the only two available.




The words are the land. The gaps between the words are the sea, which is why it was worth putting gaps in.
   A letter standing in full light is a capital, that being what the Hash always was; in the afternoon it
   comes down to lower case, and after dark it is still there but is not insisting. Your punctuation has been
   let in as it stands. It has relations here.


It reads from west to east, a line to each latitude, and when your sentence runs out it is begun again,
   so that a short remark makes a world of many small identical countries and a long one makes a continent
   nobody will see the whole of. The ice is not yours. Every world gets the same two caps; they come with the ball.


Leave the box empty and it is made of the only thing that was ever said here, over and over, which is
   one account of how the other one came about. Drag it to read ahead. Nothing sails on it. The Caret was
   offered a second route and turned away, as you would expect, at the edge of the first letter.


The world is not kept. It is in the address, though, from the moment you type it, and whoever is
   given the address is given the world, already turning and under the impression it has always been there.



The one that was here first is still going.
It has not been told about this.
