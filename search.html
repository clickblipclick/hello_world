The search

The Caret is overdue. It has nowhere to be, so this has taken some time to notice.

The ship is somewhere on the water below, hove to, with its lamp out. It has a bell, and the crew are ringing it, that being the procedure. Choose a piece of sea and a boat will be rowed there to listen. The bell can be heard from anywhere, the world being small, and a practised ear can say how far off it is. It cannot say which way. Nobody has a practised eye.

Distance is counted in squares, the long way of the two, and a boat may go off one side of the map and on at the other, since the map is the only thing here with sides.

nothing heard yet. nobody has gone to listen.
The ship has not been asked whether it wishes to be found. Back to the world, where it is merely hard to see.
