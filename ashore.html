Ashore

Nobody has been there. You are about to be the exception, on a scale that will not spoil the statistic.




You are put ashore the way anything arrives here: by being typed. You are a
lowercase i, the smallest thing that stands up, and you are in the middle of the picture. Time passes when
you do something and not otherwise. The inhabitants have always suspected as much.





  
  
  
  
  



The arrow keys also work, for those with arrows. You leave as you came, by no longer being typed.
