Off the edge

The world is forty-four characters wide. You have gone further than that.



There is nothing here, and it has not been named, which in these parts amounts to the same thing.
   The chart above is accurate. The mark is you. It drifts because you have nothing to hold on to.


The usual advice to the lost is to stay where you are until somebody comes. Nobody will,
   so the advice here is different.


The world is small and round and easy to find again.
It is this way.
