Departures

There are no ports. This is the board that would hang in one.

TIME   TO                      REMARKS
--:--  THE BOARD IS BEING WOUND

Every sailing listed is the Caret, there being no other ship, which is why the board has no column for the vessel. It was thought a column with one word in it six times would lower morale.

The times are yours. The world has its own, but a day there is over before a ticket could be torn, so the sailings have been spaced to suit somebody standing on a quay with a watch. There is no quay. Destinations are given as the crew understand them, which is in terms of what to turn away from next.

Passengers are reminded that there is no gangway, no ticket office, and no way aboard. The Caret has never left late. This is the whole of its reputation and it was not difficult to earn.

The ship itself can be watched, when it can be found. It is on the world.
